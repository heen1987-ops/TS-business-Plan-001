        <div class="govspeak"><p>GOV.UK Chat is an experimental artificial intelligence (<abbr title="artificial intelligence">AI</abbr>) feature available to users of the GOV.UK app. It is provided by the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/government-digital-service/about">Government Digital Service (<abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>)</a>, which is part of the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-science-innovation-and-technology">Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (<abbr title="Department for Science, Innovation and Technology ">DSIT</abbr>)</a>.</p>

<p>The data controller for <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr> is <abbr title="Department for Science, Innovation and Technology ">DSIT</abbr>. A data controller determines how and why personal data can be processed. </p>

<p>Mentions of ‘us’ and ‘we’ in this privacy notice refer to <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>. </p>

<p>To ask to delete data about how you’ve used GOV.UK Chat, go to section 12.</p>

<p>You can also read the <a href="/guidance/govuk-app-privacy-notice-how-we-use-your-data">GOV.UK app privacy notice</a>.</p>

<h2 id="who-this-notice-is-for">1. Who this notice is for</h2>

<p>This privacy notice is for people whose data is processed as part of providing GOV.UK Chat. </p>

<p>This includes:</p>

<ul>
  <li>anyone who has personal data published on GOV.UK (GOV.UK data subjects)</li>
  <li>users of GOV.UK Chat</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="what-data-we-collect-from-you">2. What data we collect from you</h2>

<h3 id="govuk-data-subjects">2.1 GOV.UK data subjects </h3>

<p>GOV.UK Chat relies on GOV.UK content to answer questions users may have about government and its services. GOV.UK publishes a variety of personal data as determined by the government department publishing the content (you can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/help/privacy-notice">check the GOV.UK privacy notice</a> to find out how GOV.UK collects and uses your data). </p>

<p>You may need to view the privacy notice for the department that is publishing your information to understand how and why they process and publish your personal data. We do not intend to use personal data to answer GOV.UK Chat queries. We process the personal data in order to filter and remove it from the database that GOV.UK Chat uses to generate answers. Filtering or removing is classed as processing.</p>

<h3 id="govuk-chat-users">2.2 GOV.UK Chat users</h3>

<p>When you sign into the GOV.UK app with your GOV.UK One Login credentials, we use a unique identifier code to:</p>

<ul>
  <li>understand how much you are using GOV.UK Chat</li>
  <li>group together your interactions with GOV.UK Chat to create your conversation history</li>
  <li>track and analyse how you are using GOV.UK Chat</li>
</ul>

<p>This enables you to continue conversations over multiple sessions. We also store a record of your conversations with GOV.UK Chat.</p>

<h2 id="our-legal-basis-for-processing-your-data">3. Our legal basis for processing your data</h2>

<h3 id="govuk-data-subjects-1">3.1 GOV.UK data subjects</h3>

<p>The legal basis for the processing of personal data for GOV.UK Chat is a public task, to improve how users interact with GOV.UK by evaluating the emerging technology of large language models (LLMs) (according to Article 6(1)(f) of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (<abbr title="General Data Protection Regulation">GDPR</abbr>)).</p>

<p>Some personal data published will be special category data. This is more sensitive, as defined by the UK <abbr title="General Data Protection Regulation">GDPR</abbr>. We will process special category data on the legal basis that the processing is necessary for reasons of substantial public interest, on the basis of domestic law (according to paragraph 6, schedule 1, of the Data Protection Act 2018).</p>

<h3 id="govuk-chat-users-1">3.2 GOV.UK Chat users</h3>

<p>The legal basis for the processing of personal data for GOV.UK Chat is public task (UK <abbr title="General Data Protection Regulation">GDPR</abbr> Article 6(1)(e). In this experimental phase, the basis for processing your conversation history and data linked with it is your consent (according to Article 6(1)(a).</p>

<h2 id="why-we-need-your-data">4. Why we need your data</h2>

<h3 id="govuk-data-subjects-2">4.1 GOV.UK data subjects</h3>

<p>GOV.UK Chat is designed to help users to navigate information on GOV.UK, similar to a search function. For GOV.UK Chat to provide answers to users it needs most of the data GOV.UK has to give the most accurate answer. </p>

<p>GOV.UK sometimes publishes personal data for legal reasons. To ensure GOV.UK Chat does not surface personal data, we need to process that information so we can identify and exclude it from the material it uses to answer questions. We have put in place measures to reduce the likelihood of personal data appearing in GOV.UK Chat’s answers, including:</p>

<ul>
  <li>automatically filtering out pages that are likely to contain personal data, like employment tribunal decisions, so they are not included in the database that GOV.UK Chat searches</li>
  <li>continuously refreshing the database so that up-to-date GOV.UK content is available while maintaining these filters</li>
  <li>restricting GOV.UK Chat to answering using only GOV.UK pages that remain after filtering (it does not search external sites or government service domains, and it is designed not to surface personal data)</li>
  <li>processing questions and the filtered GOV.UK content within <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>-controlled infrastructure</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="govuk-chat-users-2">4.2 GOV.UK Chat users</h3>

<p>We need your data to provide, secure and improve GOV.UK Chat. We process a small amount of personal data (a unique identifier code) linked to your GOV.UK One Login sign-in. We do this to:</p>

<ul>
  <li>assign conversation IDs to enable core features such as continuing conversations across sessions by grouping your interactions into a conversation history</li>
  <li>allow you to view and manage your conversation history</li>
  <li>understand how GOV.UK Chat is performing and make improvements, for example by checking if users got the answers they needed</li>
  <li>detect, prevent and investigate dangerous or malicious use and keep the service secure</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="what-we-do-with-your-data">5. What we do with your data</h2>

<h3 id="govuk-data-subjects-3">5.1 GOV.UK data subjects</h3>

<p>Some of the pages GOV.UK Chat has access to include personal data. This is because departments publishing on GOV.UK sometimes publish personal data for various legal reasons. If your personal data is included in these pages, it is processed as part of the GOV.UK Chat process. This process filters and removes these pages from the database accessible to the GOV.UK Chat <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr>. We only process personal data so we can identify and exclude it from any answers given to user questions. </p>

<p>To do this we:</p>

<ul>
  <li>automatically filter out pages that are likely to contain personal data, like employment tribunal decisions, so they are not included in the database that GOV.UK Chat searches</li>
  <li>continuously refresh the dataset so up-to-date content is available while maintaining these filters</li>
  <li>restrict GOV.UK Chat to answering using only the GOV.UK pages that remain after filtering (it does not search external sites or government service domains, and it is designed not to surface personal data)</li>
  <li>process only the relevant extracts from the filtered GOV.UK pages, alongside the user’s query (this input is sent to an Anthropic-trained <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> hosted on Amazon Web Services (<abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr>) within <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>-controlled accounts, which generates a natural language response)</li>
</ul>

<p>GOV.UK Chat is designed not to include personal data in responses. Anthropic does not have access to the data processed (the question and the filtered GOV.UK content used in the answer). It trains the <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> separately. The <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> then sits on a <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>-controlled <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr> account, so no data is sent to Anthropic.</p>

<p>GOV.UK Chat does not have access to: </p>

<ul>
  <li>content created on external government websites, like <a rel="external" href="https://www.ons.gov.uk/">ons.gov.uk</a>
</li>
  <li>services created by other departments, like <a rel="external" href="https://www.universal-credit.service.gov.uk/start">universal-credit.service.gov.uk</a>
</li>
  <li>any personal data you provide when using a government service, such as applying for a passport</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="govuk-chat-users-3">5.2 GOV.UK Chat users</h3>

<p>When you use GOV.UK Chat:</p>

<ul>
  <li>your question, together with relevant extracts from filtered GOV.UK content, is processed by an <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> running within <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>‑controlled <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr> accounts to generate a natural‑language answer</li>
  <li>if you include personal data in your question and it is not removed by our filters, it will be processed within our system </li>
  <li>we create and show you a conversation history</li>
  <li>we collect and analyse data from your conversation history to understand how the feature is used, improve answers, and detect and prevent dangerous or malicious use</li>
  <li>we use your unique identifier code to detect, prevent and investigate dangerous or malicious use</li>
</ul>

<p>The data we collect may be shared with other government departments, agencies and organisations.</p>

<p>We will not:</p>

<ul>
  <li>sell or rent your data to third parties</li>
  <li>share your data with third parties for marketing purposes</li>
</ul>

<p>We will share your data if we’re required to do so by law - for example, by court order, or to prevent fraud or other crime.</p>

<h2 id="how-long-we-keep-your-data">6. How long we keep your data</h2>

<h3 id="govuk-data-subjects-4">6.1 GOV.UK data subjects</h3>

<p>We will only keep your personal data for as long as:</p>

<ul>
  <li>the law requires us to</li>
  <li>we need it for the purposes listed above</li>
</ul>

<p>Personal data is filtered. This means it’s not held in the database that holds GOV.UK content which is sent to our processor to create an answer. The database is refreshed continuously to ensure up-to-date GOV.UK data is being searched. </p>

<p>For more about where your data is processed and stored, go to section 7.</p>

<h3 id="govuk-chat-users-4">6.2 GOV.UK Chat users</h3>

<p>You can clear your conversation history from your device at any time. You may wish to clear your conversation history if you share devices.</p>

<p>Clearing your conversation history does not immediately remove it from our servers. The data will remain on our servers for 12 months and is then deleted. </p>

<p>We keep your conversations, and data linked with those conversations, for 12 months and then delete them. This allows us to gain useful insights over an extended period of time so that we can:</p>

<ul>
  <li>understand if GOV.UK Chat is performing well and make sure it’s useful for users</li>
  <li>find underlying issues that our own testing has not highlighted</li>
  <li>detect, prevent and investigate dangerous or malicious use</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="where-your-data-is-processed-and-stored">7. Where your data is processed and stored</h2>

<p>For GOV.UK Chat, we use Anthropic’s Claude models hosted on <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr> Bedrock within <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>-controlled accounts. We use Google services (BigQuery and Google Analytics 4) for analytical purposes. This means any data we process is contained on equipment and systems only controlled by <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr> and run for us by <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr>. Anthropic does not store or have access to any data we process with them.</p>

<p>Data sent to <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr> and Google is processed in the UK and EU.</p>

<p>Your personal data is stored on our IT infrastructure hosted on <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr>. This means it’s shared with our data processors. Personal data stored outside the UK will be subject to equivalent legal protection through an adequacy decision. This is reliant on Standard Contractual Clauses or a UK International Data Transfer Agreement.</p>

<h2 id="who-we-share-your-data-with">8. Who we share your data with</h2>

<p>GOV.UK Chat uses an <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> from Anthropic to generate responses to user queries. When a user asks a question, GOV.UK Chat sends the query and relevant GOV.UK content to Anthropic’s <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> hosted on <abbr title="Amazon Web Services">AWS</abbr>’s infrastructure. The <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> then processes this information and produces a natural-sounding answer. The <abbr title="large language model">LLM</abbr> is within <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr>-controlled accounts. Anthropic does not have access to your data.</p>

<p>We use a service called Zendesk to allow you to give feedback about GOV.UK Chat. Only <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr> accesses the feedback you give. We delete your feedback 12 months after your last contact with us on Zendesk.</p>

<h2 id="how-we-protect-your-data-and-keep-it-secure">9. How we protect your data and keep it secure</h2>

<p>We are committed to doing all that we can to keep your data secure. We set up systems and processes to prevent unauthorised access to or disclosure of the data we collect about you – for example, we protect your data using varying levels of encryption. All third parties that process personal data for <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr> are required to keep that data secure.</p>

<h2 id="childrens-privacy-protection">10. Children’s privacy protection</h2>

<p>We do not design or promote services for children who are 13 years of age or younger, and we do not intentionally collect or keep data about anyone under the age of 13.</p>

<h2 id="your-rights">11. Your rights</h2>

<p>You have the right to request:</p>

<ul>
  <li>information about how your personal data is processed, and to request a copy of that personal data</li>
  <li>that any inaccuracies in your personal data are rectified without delay</li>
  <li>that any incomplete personal data is updated - you can include the missing information in your request</li>
  <li>that your personal data is deleted if you no longer want us to process it</li>
  <li>that the processing of your personal data is restricted in certain circumstances - for example, where accuracy is contested </li>
</ul>

<p>You also have the right to object to the processing of your personal data.</p>

<h2 id="contact-us-delete-your-data-or-make-a-complaint">12. Contact us, delete your data or make a complaint</h2>

<p>The data controller for <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr> is <abbr title="Department for Science, Innovation and Technology ">DSIT</abbr>. A data controller determines how and why personal data can be processed. </p>

<p>Contact the <abbr title="Government Digital Service">GDS</abbr> Privacy Team if you:</p>

  <li>have any questions about anything in this document</li>
  <li>think that your personal data has been misused or mishandled</li>
  <li>want to make a <a rel="external" href="https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/make-a-subject-access-request/">subject access request</a> (SAR) or other data rights request</li>
  <li>want us to delete data about how you’ve used GOV.UK Chat - if you use GOV.UK Chat after this, we will start collecting this data again</li>
</ul>
