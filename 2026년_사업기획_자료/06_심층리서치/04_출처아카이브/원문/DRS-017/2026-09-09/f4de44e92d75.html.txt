<p class="has-xx-large-font-size wp-block-paragraph">We’re using artificial intelligence to proactively find and repair faults along the railway – before they impact your journeys.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Smart tech</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We&#8217;ve been developing and using a new web application called Insight, which helps us plan and prioritise maintenance repair work and avoid delays to your journeys and freight. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Data from measurement trains, track images and remote condition monitoring all feed into Insight to create one &#8216;big picture&#8217; of the railway. Insight uses this data alongside artificial intelligence to predict and warn our maintenance teams when a fault is likely to happen. This warning can be given 28 days, 90 days or even a year before the fault is expected to occur.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A more reliable railway</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Thanks to this new tech, our maintenance and repair work is moving towards a ‘predict and prevent’ approach rather than a reactive one. We can now pre-emptively plan and fix issues before they affect the railway and your journeys.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="https://www.networkrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/NR-employee-using-Insight-on-site-1024x576.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-131923" srcset="https://www.networkrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/NR-employee-using-Insight-on-site-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://www.networkrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/NR-employee-using-Insight-on-site-300x169.jpg 300w, https://www.networkrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/NR-employee-using-Insight-on-site-768x432.jpg 768w, https://www.networkrail.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/NR-employee-using-Insight-on-site.jpg 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Figures from 2019 to 2023 show that on average, track issues result in 341 days of delays a year. Insight helps to reduce this by presenting our maintenance teams with easy-to-understand data to fix faults before they impact you or freight. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Ashley Brown, an assistant track maintenance engineer at Network Rail, said it gives “us the ability to put passengers first”. </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A safer way of working</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Using Insight helps our front line teams understand the railway and its assets right from their desk, only going to the site where necessary. This helps keep our colleagues even more safe and manage our work even more efficiently.</p>
